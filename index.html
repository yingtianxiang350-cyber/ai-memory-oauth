AI-Memory-Sync

Personal Google Drive synchronization for a private AI memory archive

What this application does

AI-Memory-Sync is a personal, self-hosted tool used by its owner to keep a private knowledge archive synchronized between Google Drive and an owner-controlled server. The archive contains documents such as study notes, creative writing references, and project records.

Why Google Drive access is requested

The owner uses rclone and a private indexing service to read and synchronize files. With the owner's authorization, synchronization and memory-management tools may create, update, organize, or delete Google Drive files. The intended working area is the owner's AI-Memory folder; however, the full Google Drive OAuth scope technically permits access beyond that folder.

Who can use it

This is a personal-use application for its owner. It is not a publicly offered cloud storage or AI service, and this website has no sign-in form.

Terms of use

This tool is provided for personal use by its owner. Changes to synchronized files may occur automatically when those features are enabled. The owner is responsible for access credentials, account security, and independent backups.

For how Google user data is accessed and handled, please read the Privacy Policy.
